ABOUT US

Who We Are

GreenRoots is a community-driven platform dedicated to restoring nature through tree planting, tracking, and collective action across India.

🎯

Our Mission

To make tree planting accessible, trackable, and impactful for every individual. We believe that when people can see the tangible results of their efforts — a seedling growing into a tree — they stay motivated to do more.

🔭

Our Vision

A future where every neighborhood is shaded by thriving trees, where communities take pride in their green legacy, and where technology bridges the gap between intention and lasting environmental action.

🌱

Our Approach

We combine simple technology with community spirit. Register your tree, track its growth over time, and share your journey with a growing network of environmental stewards.

WHY IT MATTERS

How Registration &
Tracking Helps

When you register a tree, you give it an identity. When you track its growth, you create a living record of your contribution to the planet. This data helps communities understand what's being planted, where it's thriving, and where more effort is needed.

📊 Data-Driven Impact

Tracked trees provide real data on survival rates, species distribution, and geographic coverage — helping organizations direct resources where they matter most.

🤝 Community Engagement

Public tree records inspire others to join. Seeing a neighbor's tree thrive motivates entire communities to participate in plantation drives.

🌍 Long-Term Accountability

Growth updates create a historical record. Years from now, you can look back and see the legacy you helped build — one tree at a time.
